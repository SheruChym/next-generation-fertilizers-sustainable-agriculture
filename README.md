🔬 Overview
This academic book explores innovative fertilizer technologies and their potential contribution to sustainable agriculture, improved nutrient management, and global food security.
Key Research Areas
- Next-Generation Fertilizers: Innovative approaches to fertilizer production and application.
- Nanofertilizers: Exploring nanotechnology-based nutrient delivery systems.
- Bioactive-Based Fertilizers: Investigating biostimulants and other bioactive compounds for plant growth.
- Soil Health: Understanding fertilizer effects on soil quality and nutrient availability.
- Crop Stress Resilience: Exploring technologies that may help crops cope with environmental stresses.
- Sustainable Agriculture: Improving resource-use efficiency while reducing environmental impacts.
- Food Security: Supporting sustainable agricultural productivity for a growing population.
